## Client journey

Inquiry → Discovery call → Proposal (01) → Agreement and 50% deposit (02) → Onboarding (04) → Design (2 revision rounds) → Development → Review → Final 50% payment → Launch and handoff (05) → Optional care plan (03, 06, 07)

## Sustainable workload (solo practice)

Care plans are recurring commitments. A rough capacity check before you accept new subscribers:

- Essential uses up to 0.5 h of edits plus about 0.5 h for checks and the summary, so about 1 h per client per month
- Business uses up to 2 h of edits plus about 1 h overhead, so about 3 h
- Premium uses up to 4 h of edits plus about 2 h overhead (reviews, planning), so about 6 h

Keep total maintenance commitments to a fixed share of your week (for example, no more than 30–40%) so new projects still fit. Track hours per client. A simple spreadsheet or a free tool like Toggl works.

## Recommended tools

- **Stripe**: subscriptions, invoices for project deposits (Stripe Invoicing), customer portal
- **Gmail or Google Workspace**: inquiries and client email
- **Trello, Notion or GitHub Projects**: one board per client (Requested → In progress → Review → Done)
- **Figma**: designs and prototypes
- **GitHub and Vercel**: code and deployments
- **A free e-signature tool** (for example Dropbox Sign or DocuSign free tier) for proposals and agreements
