# Maintenance request

Care plan clients can copy this into an email to [EMAIL] with the subject **"Website request: [BUSINESS NAME]"**, or you can recreate it as a Google Form.

```
Business name:
Your name:
Page(s) affected (paste the URL):
What needs to change?
  (Exact new text, or attach the file or image)
Is something broken? If so, what device and browser are you using?
Screenshot attached? (yes/no)
Priority:  ☐ Normal   ☐ Time-sensitive (needed by: ____ )
```

## How requests are handled (share with clients)

1. You will get a reply within your plan's response target during business hours, confirming the request and an estimate of the time it will use.
2. If a request would go beyond your remaining monthly editing time, you will be asked to approve the extra time ($40/hour) or schedule it for next month.
3. Larger requests (new pages, redesigns, new features) get a separate quote.
4. You will get an email when the change is live.
