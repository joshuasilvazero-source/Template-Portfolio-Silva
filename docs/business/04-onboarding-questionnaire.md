# Client onboarding questionnaire

Send after the deposit is paid. Tip: recreate it as a Google Form so answers arrive organized.

## About your business
1. Business name, as it should appear on the site:
2. In one or two sentences, what do you do and for whom?
3. Service area (cities or regions):
4. What makes you different from competitors?
5. Who are your ideal customers?

## Goals
6. What is the main thing a visitor should do on your site? (call, book, buy, request a quote, …)
7. What are 2–3 secondary goals?
8. How will you know the new site is working for you?

## Content
9. Pages you need, and what goes on each:
10. Who is writing the text? Is it ready? (Yes / Partly / No)
11. Photos: do you have professional photos of your work, team or location?
12. Logo files (SVG or high-resolution PNG):
13. Brand colors and fonts, if any:
14. Customer reviews you have permission to publish (with names, if allowed):

## Style
15. Three websites you like, and what you like about each:
16. Three words that describe how your business should feel:
17. Anything you definitely do not want?

## Technical
18. Domain name, and where it is registered:
19. Current hosting, if any:
20. Tools to connect (booking, Shopify, email signup, Google Maps, …):
21. Who needs access to the site after launch?

## Logistics
22. Main contact person and best way to reach you:
23. Hard deadlines (launch date, event, campaign):
24. Interested in a monthly care plan after launch? (Yes / Maybe / No)

## Access checklist (share securely; never by plain email)
- [ ] Domain registrar access (or a delegate invite)
- [ ] Hosting or platform access
- [ ] Existing website or CMS admin (for redesigns)
- [ ] Google Business Profile or Analytics (optional)

Use a password manager share (1Password, Bitwarden) or the platform's own "invite a collaborator" feature for credentials.
